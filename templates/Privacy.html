Privacy Policy

Last Updated: July 30, 2026

Welcome to CampusCare – College Complaint Portal. We respect your privacy and are committed to protecting your personal information. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, store, and protect the information you provide while using this portal.

1. Information We Collect

To provide complaint management services, we may collect the following information:

  • Student Name
  • College Email Address
  • Complaint Category & Description
  • Complaint ID & Status
  • Date and Time of Complaint Submission

2. How We Use Your Information

Your information is collected only for educational and administrative purposes. It is used to:

  • Verify student identity.
  • Receive and manage complaints.
  • Allow administrators to review complaints and update status.
  • Improve the complaint management process and maintain records securely.

3. Who Can Access Your Information

  • You can access only your own profile and complaints.
  • Authorized administrators can access all complaints to resolve issues.
  • Your information will not be shared with unauthorized users.

4. Data Security

We take reasonable steps to protect your information. Student accounts are created only by the college administrator, and users can access the portal only using their registered college email address and password.

  • Secure user authentication and restricted administrator access.
  • Protection against unauthorized access.
  • Regular monitoring of complaint records.

5. Complaint Confidentiality

Every complaint submitted through CampusCare is treated as confidential. Complaint details are used only for reviewing, investigating, and resolving the reported issue.

6. Acceptable Use Policy

Users are expected to use this portal responsibly. The following activities are strictly prohibited:

  • Submitting false or misleading complaints.
  • Using abusive, offensive, or threatening language.
  • Impersonating another student or staff member.
  • Attempting unauthorized access to the portal or uploading malicious content.
